YouTube: Video Agent uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to Shubham's own channel. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data deletion: remove the app at Facebook Settings -> Apps and Websites (or Google Account -> Third-party access), and the tool deletes its stored token when disconnected. Questions: contact via the Instagram account @shubham83619.
